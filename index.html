Cowboy Bebop - The Interactive Experience

2071. This year marks fifty years after an accident with a hyperspace gateway that made Earth almost uninhabitable. Humanity is forced to leave and colonize other planets and rocks in our Solar System. Crime is at an all time high, and the ISSP (Inter Solar System Police) can’t be everywhere at once and is forced to rely on Bounty Hunters to catch and turn in criminal scum. Among these bounty hunters are a special group of “Cowboys”; a rag-tag group of 5 who all live together aboard the spaceship “BEBOP”.
